# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest release receives fixes.

| Version | Supported |
| ------- | --------- |
| 1.0.x   | Yes       |
| < 1.0   | No        |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for security problems.

Report them privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**, or go directly to <https://github.com/Azazel-no/AutoBloodWeb/security/advisories/new>.

Please include:

- the app version and whether you use the installer or the portable zip;
- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.

You should get a first reply within 7 days. Once a fix is released, the advisory is published with credit to you, unless you ask not to be named.

## Scope

In scope: the AutoBloodWeb app, its installer and its logon task ("Start with DBD").

Out of scope: Dead by Daylight itself, its anti-cheat, and reports that the tool may break the game's rules (see the ban-risk warning in the README).
